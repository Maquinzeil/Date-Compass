## How to read this file

- **Listed location** means a current local business listing was found; it does not guarantee live availability or that every posted hour is correct.
- **Menu evidence** comes from an official menu/site when available, otherwise a branch-specific Foodpanda listing. Delivery-app prices may differ from dine-in prices and promotions.
- **Planner budget** below is a rough allowance for two based only on the cited items. It is not a quote, does not include transport unless stated, and should not be promoted as a guaranteed all-in price.
- **Candidate only** means venue details are not yet sufficiently verified for a firm planner recommendation.
- This pass is desk research; it does not include phone confirmation with each branch.
